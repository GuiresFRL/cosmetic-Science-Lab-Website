The science

What drives hair fall, and what cosmetics can do.

Hair fall comes from breakage along the fibre and from changes in the growth cycle. Cosmetics can address breakage, shedding and the look of density; regrowth belongs to medicines.

Topical care

Denser-looking hair, from the scalp.

Caffeine and rosemary have performed comparably to minoxidil in randomised comparative trials, and piroctone olamine increased hair amount vs placebo. We formulate light scalp serums and shampoos around them.

Clinical evidence

What the published trials show.

Peer-reviewed human studies behind the actives and approaches on this page. Each links to its record.

Randomised controlled trialHair density · caffeine

0.2% caffeine liquid vs 5% minoxidil

Randomised, open-label, multicentre non-inferiority · 210 men · 6 months

Proportion of hairs in the growth phase rose 10.6% with caffeine vs 11.7% with minoxidil, meeting the non-inferiority margin.

Minoxidil is a medicine; it appears here only as the comparator.

Dhurat R et al. Skin Pharmacol Physiol 2017;30(6):298–305 · PMID 29055953 ↗

Randomised controlled trialHair density · rosemary oil

Rosemary oil vs 2% minoxidil

Randomised comparative trial · 100 men · 6 months

Hair count rose significantly in both groups with no difference between them; scalp itching was less frequent with rosemary.

Active-comparator trial, not placebo-controlled.

Panahi Y et al. Skinmed 2015;13(1):15–21 · PMID 25842469 ↗

Randomised controlled trialHair shedding · scalp health

Piroctone olamine shampoo and leave-on vs placebo

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled · 144 women with self-perceived thinning · 8 weeks

Significant increase in hair amount vs placebo on phototrichogram; the shampoo also lowered scalp oxidative-stress markers and TEWL.

Davis MG et al. Int J Cosmet Sci 2021;43(Suppl 1):S26–S33 · PMID 34424549 ↗

Randomised controlled trialThinning hair · marine protein

Oral marine-protein supplement vs placebo

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled · 60 women · 90 days

Terminal hair count increased significantly vs placebo (P<0.0001), with less shedding and better self-assessed quality of life.

Oral supplement (nutricosmetic).

Ablon G. Dermatol Res Pract 2015;2015:841570 · PMID 25883641 ↗

Randomised controlled trialThinning hair · pumpkin seed oil

Oral pumpkin seed oil supplement (400 mg/day) vs placebo

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled · 76 men · 24 weeks

Mean hair count rose 40% vs 10% with placebo (P<0.001).

Oral supplement; the test product was a branded capsule.

Cho YH et al. Evid Based Complement Alternat Med 2014;2014:549721 · PMID 24864154 ↗

These are published studies on ingredients, methods and routines, not on products we have made. A finished product needs its own testing before a claim goes on pack, and we design that study with you. See the full evidence library

Cosmetic or drug?

Regrowth claims, and actives such as minoxidil, make a product a medicine in most markets. Cosmetic claims focus on breakage, thickness and appearance.

FAQ

Hair fall & hair growth questions.

Can a cosmetic claim to regrow hair?

No. Regrowth and alopecia claims are medicinal in most markets. Cosmetic claims cover breakage, shedding and the look of density.

How long does a hair-fall study take?

Usually 12 to 24 weeks, because the growth cycle is slow.

Can you develop supplements too?

Yes, through our group’s food and nutraceutical team, with the right regulatory route.

Who owns the formula?

You do. We sign an NDA before you share your brief, and the formula and technical file are handed over at the end of the project.
