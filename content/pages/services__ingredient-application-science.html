Application studies

The ingredient tested in real finished bases such as serums, creams, cleansers, shampoos, sunscreens and colour, to show where it performs and where it does not.

Questions

Asked before we start.

Who owns the data?

You do. Results, formulas and reports are delivered to you, and CSL does not publish or share them without your written permission.

Can you test against a competitor ingredient?

Yes. Comparators are bought on the open market and tested under the same conditions, with results reported as measured.

Do you also run human studies?

Yes. Instrumental and panel studies can follow the lab work through the CSL claims and clinical service.

How long does a study take?

A first application screen usually takes 4 to 8 weeks; stability adds 12 weeks for an accelerated read.
