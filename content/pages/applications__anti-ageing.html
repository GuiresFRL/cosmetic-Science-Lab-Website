The science

How skin ages, and what we formulate against.

Visible ageing comes from within (collagen loss over time) and from outside (UV, pollution). We target both.

Collagen & wrinkles

Smoother skin, measured in weeks.

Retinoids, vitamin C and niacinamide are among the best-studied cosmetic actives for fine lines, with vehicle-controlled trials showing improvement over 12 to 24 weeks. Bakuchiol offers a gentler alternative with comparable results in a head-to-head trial.

Formulation note

Retinoids and vitamin C are unstable. We protect them with the right pH, antioxidants and airless or opaque packs, and test potency through shelf life.

Prevention

The best anti-ageing is daily SPF.

In a 4.5-year randomised trial, daily sunscreen users showed 24% less skin ageing. Pairing daily SPF with antioxidants and hydration makes prevention part of the routine.

Clinical evidence

What the published trials show.

Peer-reviewed human studies behind the actives and approaches on this page. Each links to its record.

Randomised controlled trialAnti-ageing · retinol

0.4% retinol lotion vs vehicle

Randomised, double-blind, vehicle-controlled · 36 older adults · 24 weeks

Fine wrinkling improved significantly vs vehicle (score change −1.64 vs −0.08, P<.001), with increased glycosaminoglycans and procollagen I.

Kafi R et al. Arch Dermatol 2007;143(5):606–612 · PMID 17515510 ↗

Randomised controlled trialAnti-ageing · niacinamide

5% niacinamide vs vehicle, split-face

Double-blind, split-face · 50 women · 12 weeks

Significant reductions in fine lines, wrinkles, spots, red blotchiness and sallowness, and improved elasticity, vs the vehicle side.

Bissett DL et al. Dermatol Surg 2005;31(7 Pt 2):860–865 · PMID 16029679 ↗

Randomised controlled trialAnti-ageing · vitamin C

5% vitamin C cream vs vehicle

Double-blind, randomised, split-site · 20 women · 6 months

Clinical photoageing score improved from 6.7 to 4.4 on the treated side, with denser skin microrelief and fewer deep furrows.

Humbert PG et al. Exp Dermatol 2003;12(3):237–244 · PMID 12823436 ↗

Randomised controlled trialAnti-ageing · bakuchiol

0.5% bakuchiol vs 0.5% retinol

Randomised, double-blind · 44 adults · 12 weeks

Both reduced wrinkle area and hyperpigmentation with no significant difference; bakuchiol caused less scaling and stinging.

Dhaliwal S et al. Br J Dermatol 2019;180(2):289–296 · PMID 29947134 ↗

Randomised controlled trialHydration · hyaluronic acid

0.1% hyaluronic acid creams (50–2000 kDa) vs vehicle

Randomised, controlled, intra-individual · 76 women · 60 days

All molecular weights improved hydration and elasticity vs vehicle; the low weights (50 and 130 kDa) also reduced wrinkle depth.

Pavicic T et al. J Drugs Dermatol 2011;10(9):990–1000 · PMID 22052267 ↗

Randomised controlled trialPhotoageing · daily sunscreen

Daily broad-spectrum SPF 15+ vs discretionary use

Randomised community trial, blinded assessment · 903 adults · 4.5 years

Daily users showed 24% less skin ageing (relative odds 0.76) than discretionary users.

Hughes MC et al. Ann Intern Med 2013;158(11):781–790 · PMID 23732711 ↗

These are published studies on ingredients, methods and routines, not on products we have made. A finished product needs its own testing before a claim goes on pack, and we design that study with you. See the full evidence library

Cosmetic or drug?

Anti-ageing claims stay cosmetic when they describe appearance. Claims about changing skin structure or collagen production can push a product towards medicine rules in some markets.

FAQ

Anti-ageing & wrinkles questions.

How long does an anti-ageing study take?

Most instrumental studies run 8 to 12 weeks; claims about collagen or deep wrinkles often need 12 to 24 weeks.

Can you use retinol in products for the EU?

Yes, within the EU limits introduced for retinol and retinyl esters in face and body products, with the required labelling.

Is bakuchiol as good as retinol?

One randomised trial found similar results on wrinkles and pigmentation with less irritation. We use it where gentleness matters.

Who owns the formula?

You do. We sign an NDA before you share your brief, and the formula and technical file are handed over at the end of the project.
