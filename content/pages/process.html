How Guires CSL takes you from brief to batch

How long does cosmetic product development take? A full project typically takes 21 to 36 weeks from brief to pilot batch, and less if you already have a formula. Here is every stage, with typical week ranges.

The stages

What happens at each stage.

  1. Brief & concept1–2 weeks (typical)Target consumer, benchmark, claims wish-list, markets, pack and target cost agreed in a written brief. More
  2. Formulation4–8 weeks (typical)Ingredients screened for each market, bench trials and a shortlist of formulas. More
  3. Prototype2–6 weeks (typical)Sample rounds, sensory refinement, fragrance, colour and packaging until you sign off a golden sample. More
  4. Testing12+ weeks (typical)Accelerated stability gives a first read at about 12 weeks; challenge, micro and safety work run alongside. More
  5. Claims & clinical support3–12 weeks (typical)Patch tests, use trials and efficacy studies, scoped to the claims. Usually runs in parallel with stability. More
  6. Regulatory2–8 weeks per market (typical)Safety reports, notifications and label review for each market on your list. More
  7. Pilot & scale-up3–6 weeks (typical)Micro-batches for launch, then tech transfer to full production. More

Before your first call

What to send us.

The more we know, the sharper the proposal. None of it is required to start.

  • Product type and format
  • Target consumer and skin or hair type
  • A benchmark product, if you have one
  • Claims you would like on pack
  • Launch markets
  • Pack type and size
  • Target retail price or cost per unit
  • Launch date

You own your formula. We sign an NDA before you share your brief, and the formula and its documentation are yours.
