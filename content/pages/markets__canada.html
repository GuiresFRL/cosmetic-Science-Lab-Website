Questions

Asked before we start.

Is there pre-market approval for cosmetics in Canada?

No. You notify Health Canada within ten days of first sale, but the product must already comply.

Do labels have to be bilingual?

Yes, most mandatory information must be in English and French. The INCI list does not need translating.

Are sunscreens cosmetics in Canada?

No. They are drugs or natural health products, depending on the ingredients.
