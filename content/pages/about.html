Guires Cosmetic Science Lab

The cosmetic R&D centre of the Guires Group

Guires Cosmetic Science Lab (Guires CSL) is the cosmetic and cosmeceutical R&D centre of the Guires Group, sister to Food Research Lab. From the Guires R&D Innovation Centre in Chennai, our formulation scientists, analysts, toxicologists and regulatory specialists take beauty and personal care products from first brief to pilot batch, with clinical trials and market files for brands worldwide.

Our story & mission

Grounded in science, guided by integrity.

Cosmetic Science Lab grew from a simple belief: brands of every size deserve the same quality of formulation science, testing and regulatory care that the largest companies take for granted. Built on 15 years of product development at the Guires R&D Innovation Centre, we bring that depth to cosmetics and personal care.

Our mission is to help brands launch products that work, that meet the rules of every market they sell in, and that customers trust, by grounding every decision in published evidence and transparent data.

Who we are

One platform, a dedicated cosmetics team.

Part of the Guires R&D Innovation Centre

The Guires R&D Innovation Centre in Chennai is also home to Food Research Lab, a contract research, development and pilot manufacturing organisation serving food, nutraceutical and cosmeceutical brands worldwide. Cosmetic Science Lab shares its labs, methods and experience, applied to cosmetics.

Facilities

Formulation labs, cosmeceutical and microbiology laboratories, sensory booths, and pilot manufacturing and scale-up units.

Global reach

Clients across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and Asia-Pacific, with every project coordinated centrally.

Track record

15 years in product development, with clients in 10+ countries.

Cruelty-free by method

No animal testing. Safety work uses validated in-vitro models, ingredient data and supervised human studies.

A word from our founder

Good science, done honestly, is the surest route to a product people trust. Get the foundations right and great innovation follows.

I founded Guires in 2009 with a team of three and a passion for research and analytics. Before that I worked with WHO SEARO and the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), served as visiting faculty at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, and supported several global pharmaceutical clients. Since then the group has grown into six specialist businesses serving researchers, life science companies and consumer brands worldwide.

I founded Cosmetic Science Lab at the Guires R&D Innovation Centre to bring that same research depth to beauty and personal care, giving brands one development partner from the first formula to the finished product. As Chairman of the group, I set its vision and long-term strategy and lead its two divisions, Guires Research Services and Guires Labs, through their chairpersons.

At CSL, I guide the leadership team, the heads of formulation, regulatory affairs, and testing and clinical studies, on direction, scientific standards and client commitments. They lead the day-to-day work with brands, supported by multidisciplinary teams of scientists, clinicians, writers, statisticians and lab specialists in Chennai and Manchester. Together we hold to a culture of evidence, integrity and client partnership, so every product, claim and market file is built on evidence you can check.

Formulation lab

Where your formula takes shape, from the first bench trial to a sample worth testing.

  • Concept and formula development
  • Bench trials and ingredient swaps
  • Samples prepared for your review and panels

Cosmeceutical lab

Specialist work on active-led skin and hair care, where stability and delivery decide performance.

  • Actives, pH and delivery systems
  • Emulsions, serums, gels and balms
  • Reverse engineering of benchmarks

Microbiology lab

Checks that every product stays safe from contamination, batch after batch.

  • Preservative efficacy (ISO 11930)
  • Microbial limits for batch release
  • Environmental monitoring

Stability & QC

Where formulas are aged, measured and released against specification.

  • Accelerated and real-time stability
  • pH, viscosity and physical checks
  • Packaging compatibility

Sensory booths

Controlled booths where trained panels judge texture, feel and fragrance.

  • Spreadability, absorption and after-feel
  • Fragrance and colour evaluation
  • Consumer perception panels

Pilot manufacturing

Micro-batches made to the signed-off process, ready for launch or market tests.

  • Small batch, low MOQ runs
  • Scale-up trials
  • Process notes for tech transfer

Packaging & filling

Filling and packing into the format your product will ship in.

  • Filling into your chosen pack
  • Compatibility checks
  • Labelling for pilot batches

Ingredient store

A controlled store for raw materials, with traceability from supplier to batch.

  • CoA-verified raw materials
  • Batch traceability
  • Allergen and halal segregation where needed
