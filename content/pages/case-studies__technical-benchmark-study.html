Case study · Technical benchmarking

A technical benchmark study of a client’s new range: proving it against the market, without animal testing

A cosmetic brand in Canada had developed three new products and asked CSL to show how they measure up. CSL benchmarked each one against several commercially available reference products, covering toxicology, safety, mode of action, efficacy and product attributes such as design and colour.

01

The brief

Three new products to prove against the market

The client had already developed three products for the Canadian market: a skin brightening and anti-ageing product, a men’s hair care product and a concentrated shampoo paste. Before launch, the team wanted independent proof of how each one compared with what shoppers already buy.

The client’s rule was simple: each product had to at least match the quality of the best products on the market. CSL was asked to run a technical benchmark study of all three, choosing the right reference products for each and comparing them parameter by parameter.

  • Client product 2Men’s hair care
  • Client product 3Concentrated shampoo paste
02

The challenge

Safety benchmarks without animal data

Benchmarking safety used to lean on animal data. That route is now closed. The EU bans testing cosmetic ingredients and finished products on animals, and since March 2013 it also bans the sale of cosmetics developed with new animal tests. Canada brought in its own ban on cosmetic animal testing in December 2023.

What the team had to work with

  1. No animal testingSafety had to be shown with in vitro methods, existing data and expert assessment.
  2. Limited public dataThe full composition, ingredient levels and safety data of the market reference products are rarely in the public domain.
  3. Many parametersEach client product had to be compared on toxicology, safety, mode of action and efficacy, and on attributes such as design and colour, and no single reference product covers all of them.

The task was to build benchmarks robust enough for real safety and launch decisions, using only animal-free evidence and incomplete information about the reference products.

03

Our approach

Benchmarks built the way toxicologists work

CSL’s deformulation and safety team designed the study around the way toxicologists use established products as reference points, and selected several reference products for each client product, depending on what each parameter needed.

  1. Step 1

    Select the references

    For each client product, the team chose several commercially available reference products, picking different references for toxicology, safety, mode of action, efficacy and product attributes such as design and colour. Where the composition, ingredient levels or safety data of a reference were not public, assumptions were based on its manufacturer’s own safety and efficacy claims.

  2. Step 2

    Bracket with more than one benchmark

    Each client product was set against an acceptable benchmark, and against borderline references where useful, to narrow down where it sits on the safety spectrum. Using several reference points is common practice, depending on the existing data and the purpose of the study.

  3. Step 3

    Bring in analogues

    Where an ingredient under review was a chemical such as a surfactant, a structurally similar analogue, or one from the same chemical or functional class with known toxicology data, was used as a reference.

  4. Step 4

    Calibrate with reference standards

    In vitro skin irritation reference standards, whose irritation classification is already established, covered both ends of the profile, irritant and non-irritant. This calibrated the study and gave a measure of confidence in each classification.

04 · The result

Clear evidence of where each product stands

The client received one detailed report for all three products: toxicology, safety and mode of action against the benchmarks, the efficacy evidence for each product’s intended use, product attributes such as design and colour, and the calibration against reference standards. The client was pleased with the depth of the report, which gave the team clear evidence of where each product stands against the market.

What the client received

Benchmark toxicologyEach client product’s toxicological profile set against its reference products
Safety assessmentSafety for the intended use, built on animal-free evidence
Mode of action and efficacyHow each product works, and the evidence behind its efficacy against the benchmarks
Attributes and calibrationDesign and colour comparison, and in vitro irritation results calibrated against irritant and non-irritant standards
05

Why it matters

Benchmarking that drives decisions

Reference products and standards with known profiles make it possible to judge whether a new formula is acceptable, which helps brands make launch decisions and keep innovating without animal testing.

A measurable bar

Each product is judged against defined benchmarks for every parameter, not a general impression of the market leader.

Animal-free confidence

Calibrated in vitro data supports safety decisions that meet EU and Canadian animal testing bans.

Launch with evidence

Gaps against the market are found before launch, so the team knows exactly where a product leads and where to improve it.
