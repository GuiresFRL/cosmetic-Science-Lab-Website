Glossary · Labelling & formulation

ISO 16128

What it means

The ISO guidelines on technical definitions and criteria for natural and organic cosmetic ingredients (Part 1, 2016) and the way to calculate natural and organic origin indexes for ingredients and products (Part 2, 2017). The standard defines how to calculate the indexes; it does not set claim thresholds or replace certification schemes such as COSMOS.

Sources: 12

Why it matters

Getting this right early shapes the formula, the label and the claims, and avoids costly reformulation once a product is close to launch.

How Guires CSL applies it

Guires CSL formulation, testing, clinical and regulatory teams work with ISO 16128 on client projects every week, from the first brief to the final product file.

INCI (International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients)

The standard ingredient names used on cosmetic labels in the EU, US, ASEAN and many other markets, assigned by the INCI committee of the Personal Care Products Council.

pH

A measure of acidity or alkalinity on a scale of 0 to 14.

Read definition →

Emulsion

A dispersion of oil and water held together by emulsifiers, either oil-in-water (most lotions and creams) or water-in-oil (richer, water-resistant textures).

Rheology

How a product flows and deforms: viscosity, yield stress and shear thinning.
