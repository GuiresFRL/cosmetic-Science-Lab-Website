Who we are

Science at every step, from first formula to finished product.

Cosmetic Science Lab is a cosmetic product development company for beauty and personal care brands. CSL formulation chemists, testing scientists and regulatory specialists handle personal care product development end to end: custom cosmetic formulation, cosmetic testing, claims and clinical trial support, cosmetic regulatory services and small batch manufacturing. As an evidence-based cosmetic contract research organisation (CRO) at the Guires R&D Innovation Centre, CSL runs cosmetic and cosmeceutical R&D, clinical trials and pilot manufacturing under one roof.

Custom formulation, private label or white label?

Choose the route that fits your brand.

Most brands compare a cosmetic manufacturer’s private label range with bespoke custom formulation. Here is the honest difference, and where we fit.

RouteWhat you getFormula ownershipMinimum orderBest for
White labelAn existing stock formula and pack with your logoThe manufacturer’sLowTesting a market quickly
Private labelA stock formula from an ODM or contract manufacturer, lightly adaptedUsually the manufacturer’sSet by the factory, often 1,000–5,000 unitsFast launches with a proven base
Custom formulation (us)A bespoke formula built by a cosmetic chemist to your brief, tested and registeredYoursLow MOQ: small batch sized to your requirementBrands that want a unique product and freedom to switch manufacturer

Unlike an ODM or CDMO that ties the formula to its own factory, we hand over a full tech-transfer pack so any contract manufacturer can make your product. Want speed? We can start from a proven, ready-to-launch base formula and adapt it, turnkey from brief to finished product.

How we work

Seven stages, one team, concept to shelf.

Join at any stage. Most brands start with a brief; some arrive with a formula that needs testing, claims or a market file. We pick it up from there.

    Brief & concept

    Prototype

    2–6 wks

  4. 04

    12+ wks

  5. 05

    Pilot & scale-up

    3–6 wks

Market-by-market detail

Non-animal safety methods.

Cosmetic animal testing is banned in the EU, UK, India and a growing list of markets. Our safety work relies on validated in-vitro models, ingredient safety data and supervised human studies, so your product qualifies everywhere.
