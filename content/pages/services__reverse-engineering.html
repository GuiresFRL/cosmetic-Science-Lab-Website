Cosmetic Reverse Engineering from Guires CSL

At Guires Cosmetic Science Lab, CSL formulation chemists offer cosmetic reverse engineering and benchmark matching: identifying every ingredient by GC-MS, HPLC, FT-IR, NMR and ICP-MS, profiling the fragrance by HS- and SPME-GC-MS, and estimating % w/w ranges at the Guires R&D Innovation Centre, then building your own manufacturable formula that matches the benchmark’s texture and performance.

Questions

Asked before we start.

Is reverse engineering legal?

Analysing a product you have bought is generally lawful. Copying a patented formula or process may not be, so CSL includes a patent check before launch, and the new formula is developed independently.

Will the result be an exact copy?

No. The goal is a formula that matches the benchmark’s performance and feel, built with your own choice of ingredients and cost, and owned by you.

Can we market it as a dupe of a named brand?

CSL advises against using competitor brand names in marketing. Describe your product on its own merits and claims you can prove.

Can you identify every ingredient?

In most products, yes. GC-MS, HPLC, FT-IR, NMR and ICP-MS identify oils, silicones, polymers, actives, preservatives and trace metals, and HS-GC-MS and SPME-GC-MS profile the fragrance. Concentrations are reported as estimated % w/w ranges, because some ingredients, such as complex polymers and botanical extracts, can only be estimated.
