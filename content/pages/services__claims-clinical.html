Questions

Asked before we start.

Are you a clinical research organisation?

No. We are a product development partner. Clinical trials are one step we plan and coordinate for your product, alongside formulation, testing and regulatory work.

Can I make a claim without a clinical study?

Often, yes. Many claims can be supported by ingredient data, in-vitro tests or consumer perception studies. The claim finder above shows typical routes.

How many volunteers does a study need?

It depends on the endpoint. Instrumental efficacy studies often start around 20 volunteers; HRIPT panels typically run from 50 to 200.
