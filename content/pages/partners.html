How it works

From application to first project.

  1. ApplyComplete the form below with your organisation, expertise and credentials.
  2. Introductory callWe learn about your capabilities, capacity and the projects you want.
  3. VerificationWe check registrations, accreditations and references.
  4. AgreementConfidentiality and partnership terms agreed in writing.
  5. Listed in the partner networkYou receive project requests that match your expertise.

What to prepare

Credentials we ask for.

  • Dermatologists: medical registration number and specialty
  • Laboratories: accreditation certificates and scope (ISO 17025, GLP, GCP)
  • Ethics committee registration, for clinical sites
  • Contract manufacturers: ISO 22716 / GMP certificates
  • Regulatory partners: Responsible Person or agent status per market
  • Universities: department and programme contact
