Cosmeceuticals in the UAE: cosmetic or drug?

High-performance skin care sits on the line between cosmetic and medicine. In the UAE, where that line falls decides which authority registers your product and how long it takes.

Executive summary

“Cosmeceutical” is not a legal category in the UAE. Appearance claims keep a product in cosmetics; treatment claims and some actives push it towards the medicinal route, with longer timelines.

Take-home message for manufacturers

  • Screen every active and its level against GSO and UAE lists
  • Keep formulas within cosmetic limits for restricted actives
  • Prepare halal documentation where retailers expect it

Take-home message for brand owners

  • Write claims about appearance, not treatment
  • Keep Arabic and English claims consistent
  • Confirm the registration route before you print

“Cosmeceutical” is not a legal category

Regulators do not recognise “cosmeceutical”. A product is either a cosmetic or a medicinal or health product. The deciding factors are what the product claims to do and what it contains.

Claims decide the route

Claims about appearance (“reduces the look of fine lines”, “for blemish-prone skin”) keep a product in cosmetics. Claims about treating or preventing a condition (“treats acne”, “stops hair loss”, “heals eczema”) point towards the medicinal route, with the federal drug regulator rather than the cosmetics registration system.

Ingredients can too

Some actives are restricted to medicines, or allowed in cosmetics only at low levels. Hydroquinone, high-strength retinoids and certain antifungals are typical examples. Check each active against GSO standards and the UAE’s current restricted lists before you finalise the formula.

What to do in practice

  • Write claims as appearance claims if you want the cosmetic route
  • Screen actives and levels against GSO and UAE lists
  • Keep Arabic label content consistent with the English claims
  • Plan halal documentation if your retailers expect it
  • Check the registration route with the authority before you print

Questions readers ask

Is “cosmeceutical” a registration category in the UAE?

No. A product is registered either as a cosmetic or as a medicinal or health product.

What decides the route?

Mainly the claims you make and the actives you use.
