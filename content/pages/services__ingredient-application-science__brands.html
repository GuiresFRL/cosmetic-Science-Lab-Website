Questions

Asked before we start.

Can you test an active the supplier has only just launched?

Yes. Send the supplier’s sample and documentation; CSL checks performance in your base and the regulatory status in your markets.

Will the supplier see our results?

No. Results belong to you and are not shared with suppliers unless you ask.

Does this replace product development?

No. It answers the ingredient question first, so product development starts with the right active at the right level.
