Custom formulation, private label or white label?

Choose the route that fits your brand.

Most brands compare a cosmetic manufacturer’s private label range with bespoke custom formulation. Here is the honest difference, and where we fit.

RouteWhat you getFormula ownershipMinimum orderBest for
White labelAn existing stock formula and pack with your logoThe manufacturer’sLowTesting a market quickly
Private labelA stock formula from an ODM or contract manufacturer, lightly adaptedUsually the manufacturer’sSet by the factory, often 1,000–5,000 unitsFast launches with a proven base
Custom formulation (us)A bespoke formula built by a cosmetic chemist to your brief, tested and registeredYoursLow MOQ: small batch sized to your requirementBrands that want a unique product and freedom to switch manufacturer

Unlike an ODM or CDMO that ties the formula to its own factory, we hand over a full tech-transfer pack so any contract manufacturer can make your product. Want speed? We can start from a proven, ready-to-launch base formula and adapt it, turnkey from brief to finished product.

Questions

Asked by first-time founders.

How do I develop a skin care product from scratch?

Start with a brief: the customer, the benefit, the markets and a target price. We turn that into a formula, samples, testing, claims and a market file, step by step.

How does your pricing work? How much does it cost to develop a skincare product?

It depends on the product, the claims and the markets. We quote a fixed fee per stage (formulation, testing, claims, regulatory, pilot), so you only pay for the stages you need. Ask for an indicative scope and we reply within one working day.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our micro-batch runs are sized to your requirement, so there is no factory-style MOQ. When you scale, we transfer the formula to a contract manufacturer whose minimum order suits your volumes.

Do I own the formula and the IP?

Yes. The formula, its documentation, your brief and the intellectual property (IP) in the product are yours, and we sign an NDA before you share anything.

Private label or custom formulation: which is right for my first product?

Private label is faster but the formula usually belongs to the manufacturer. Custom formulation takes longer, but the product is unique and yours to move.

Can I use my own packaging?

Yes. Send us the pack and we test compatibility during stability testing.

Can you work with a formula I already have?

Yes. We can review it, test it, fix stability issues, and run a formula transfer to your manufacturer.

How do I request samples?

Send your brief through the enquiry form and tick the sample request option. Sample rounds are part of the prototype stage.

I am not a chemist. Do I need to hire a cosmetic chemist?

No. Your project is led by CSL formulation chemists. You bring the idea, the consumer and the price point.
