Moisturiser Formulations from Guires CSL

At Guires Cosmetic Science Lab, CSL formulation chemists develop moisturiser formulations and barrier-repair products with glycerin, hyaluronic acid and ceramides, with claims such as 24-hour hydration proven by Corneometer and TEWL studies. With more than 10 years of experience, the team develops formulas that measurably improve skin hydration and barrier function using an evidence-based approach. TEWL measures the water lost through the stratum corneum, the outer layer of the skin barrier, so a fall in TEWL shows the barrier recovering.

The science

How skin stays hydrated.

Hydration depends on water-binding humectants inside the skin and a lipid barrier that stops it escaping. We formulate for both.

Humectants

Water drawn in, and held.

Glycerin and hyaluronic acid bind water in the outer skin layer. Randomised trials show glycerin raises hydration within a week, and that lower-weight hyaluronic acid also improves elasticity.

  • CorneometerHydration at 1, 8, 24 and 72 hours

Published evidenceBreternitz 2008 · glycerinPavicic 2011 · hyaluronic acidLodén 2002 · glycerin vs urea
Claims note

24-hour or 72-hour claims need kinetic measurements at those time points. We set them before the study begins.

Barrier lipids

A barrier that keeps water in.

Ceramides, cholesterol and fatty acids rebuild the lipid layers between skin cells. Controlled trials show ceramide creams lower water loss and keep working after use stops.

CeramidesCholesterolFree fatty acidsSqualaneNiacinamideEctoin

How CSL proves it works

  • TEWLTransepidermal water loss before and after use
  • Tape-strip challengeRecovery after controlled barrier stress
  • Dryness scoringExpert grading over 2–4 weeks
Published evidenceLueangarun 2019 · ceramidesWeber 2012 · body lotionMarini 2014 · ectoin
Formulation note

Barrier lipids need the right ratio and structure. We use lamellar or cream-gel bases that hold them in skin-like layers.

Clinical evidence

What the published trials show.

Peer-reviewed human studies behind the actives and approaches on this page. Each links to its record.

Randomised controlled trialHydration · glycerin

20% glycerol emollient vs vehicle

Double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised · 24 adults with atopic dermatitis · 4 weeks

Skin hydration rose vs vehicle from week 1 (p<0.0001) and barrier function (TEWL) improved by week 4.

Breternitz M et al. Skin Pharmacol Physiol 2008;21(1):39–45 · PMID 18025807 ↗

Randomised controlled trialHydration · hyaluronic acid

0.1% hyaluronic acid creams (50–2000 kDa) vs vehicle

Randomised, controlled, intra-individual · 76 women · 60 days

All molecular weights improved hydration and elasticity vs vehicle; the low weights (50 and 130 kDa) also reduced wrinkle depth.

Pavicic T et al. J Drugs Dermatol 2011;10(9):990–1000 · PMID 22052267 ↗

Randomised controlled trialDry skin · glycerin vs urea

Glycerin cream vs urea-saline cream

Randomised, double-blind · 197 adults with atopic dermatitis · 30 days

Both relieved dryness equally; the glycerin cream caused less stinging (10% vs 24% moderate–severe).

Lodén M et al. Acta Derm Venereol 2002;82(1):45–47 · PMID 12013198 ↗

Randomised controlled trialBarrier · ceramides

Ceramide cream vs hydrophilic cream, split-body

Double-blind, randomised · 24 adults with dry skin · 28 days + 7-day follow-up

Better hydration, lower TEWL and improved skin pH at every time point, maintained 7 days after stopping.

Lueangarun S et al. Dermatol Ther 2019;32(6):e13090 · PMID 31585489 ↗

Controlled trialDry skin · body lotion

Glyceryl glucoside, NMF and ceramide lotion vs vehicle

Double-blind, vehicle-controlled studies · 169 adults · 2 weeks

Hydration rose and TEWL fell significantly vs vehicle and untreated skin; the richer lotion still moisturised 6 days after stopping.

Weber TM et al. J Clin Aesthet Dermatol 2012;5(8):29–39 · PMID 22916312 ↗

Randomised controlled trialBarrier · ectoin

Ectoin cream vs a non-steroidal barrier cream

Randomised, double-blind, intra-individual, multicentre · 65 adults · 28 days

Efficacy and tolerability equivalent to the active comparator on eczema score, global assessment and itch.

Marini A et al. Skin Pharmacol Physiol 2014;27(2):57–65 · PMID 23949258 ↗

These are published studies on ingredients, methods and routines, not on products we have made. A finished product needs its own testing before a claim goes on pack, and we design that study with you. See the full evidence library

FAQ

Hydration & barrier repair questions.

What is the difference between hydrating and moisturising?

Hydrating ingredients add water to skin; moisturising also includes lipids that seal it in. Most products need both.

How do you prove a barrier-repair claim?

With TEWL measurements, often after a controlled barrier challenge, on panels over two to four weeks.

Can hydration claims be made on body products?

Yes, with the same Corneometer methods, usually on the lower leg or forearm.

Who owns the formula?

You do. We sign an NDA before you share your brief, and the formula and technical file are handed over at the end of the project.
