The science

What causes flakes.

Dandruff links to an overgrowth of Malassezia yeast, scalp oil and an irritated barrier. We address all three.

Flake control

Visibly fewer flakes, wash after wash.

Anti-dandruff actives reduce the yeast that drives flaking. Piroctone olamine shampoo has also improved scalp barrier and oxidative-stress markers in a placebo-controlled trial.

Market note

Zinc pyrithione has been banned in EU cosmetics since 2022, and US dandruff shampoos are OTC drugs limited to permitted actives. We often build separate EU and US versions.

Scalp microbiome

A rebalanced scalp, from inside and out.

A randomised, placebo-controlled trial found an oral probiotic reduced flakes and redness and rebalanced scalp microbiota. Topically, prebiotics and mild bases support the scalp’s own flora.

Cosmetic or drug?

Anti-dandruff products are OTC drugs in the US. In the EU, zinc pyrithione is now banned in cosmetics, so we build on permitted alternatives.

FAQ

Dandruff & scalp health questions.

Can we sell the same anti-dandruff shampoo in the EU and US?

Usually not. The US requires an OTC drug active such as pyrithione zinc, which is now banned in EU cosmetics. We develop market-specific versions.

How do you prove a flake-reduction claim?

With expert flake grading on panels with dandruff, before and after two to four weeks of use.

Can dandruff care be microbiome-friendly?

Yes, by balancing the active with mild surfactants and prebiotics, and testing with scalp swabs.

Who owns the formula?

You do. We sign an NDA before you share your brief, and the formula and technical file are handed over at the end of the project.
