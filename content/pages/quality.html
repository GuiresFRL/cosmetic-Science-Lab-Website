Guires Cosmetic Science Lab

Quality you can check and defend

Quality at Guires CSL starts at the bench and runs through every batch: a certified quality management system, documented methods, calibrated instruments, traceable raw materials and controlled records, so every result we report can be checked and defended.

Start your project with Guires CSL →

Raw materials

Quality starts at the source

Every formula is only as good as what goes into it. Guires CSL qualifies suppliers before their materials reach the bench, checks each lot against its specification and certificate of analysis, and keeps raw materials in a controlled store with quarantine and release status.

  • Supplier qualification and approved supplier list
  • Certificate of analysis checked for every lot
  • Allergen, halal and vegan segregation where needed
  • Lot traceability from supplier to finished batch

Testing

Quality at the bench

Results are only useful if they can be repeated and defended. Guires CSL runs documented methods to ISO and OECD standards on calibrated, maintained instruments, with controls in every run and raw data kept for review.

  • ISO 11930, ISO 17516 and OECD test methods
  • Instrument calibration and maintenance records
  • Positive and negative controls in every run
  • Raw data and reports kept for audit

Pilot manufacturing

Quality in every batch

Pilot and small batches follow ISO 22716 cosmetic GMP guidelines and the process signed off in development. Each batch is recorded, sampled and checked against its release specification before it leaves the Guires R&D Innovation Centre.

  • Batch manufacturing records for every run
  • In-process and release testing
  • Retained samples for every batch
  • Change control when a formula or supplier changes

Our people

A team you can trust

Quality is part of how the Guires CSL team works, not a final check. Formulation scientists, analysts, microbiologists, toxicologists and regulatory specialists are trained on the procedures they use, and every report is reviewed before it reaches you.

  • Role-based training records
  • Second-person review of every report
  • Qualified safety assessors for safety reports
  • Clear ownership from brief to batch

Customer focus

Need certificates, a quality questionnaire completed or an audit of how your project is run? The Guires CSL quality team supports your due diligence and supplier approval.

Regulatory support

Rules differ by market and change often. Guires CSL regulatory specialists link test data to the safety files, claims and labels each market expects, from the EU CPSR to MoCRA.

Clinical standards

Every human study runs to Good Clinical Practice, with independent ethics committee approval, informed consent and dermatologist supervision where the study design calls for it.
