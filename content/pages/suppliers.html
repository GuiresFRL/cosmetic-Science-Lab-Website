How it works

From application to first RFQ.

  1. ApplyComplete the form below with your company, categories and certifications.
  2. Documentation reviewWe review CoA, SDS, TDS, certifications and regulatory statements.
  3. QualificationSample evaluation, and an audit for critical materials where needed.
  4. Listed in the directoryYour company and materials are listed for our formulation and sourcing teams.
  5. Briefs and RFQsYou receive sampling requests and quotation requests that match your categories.

What to prepare

Documents we ask for.

Have these ready to speed up qualification.

  • Certificate of Analysis (CoA)
  • Safety Data Sheet (SDS)
  • Technical Data Sheet (TDS)
  • INCI name and CAS number
  • Allergen and IFRA statements (fragrance)
  • Regulatory statements: REACH, origin, animal testing, CMR, nanomaterials
  • Certifications: ISO 22716, ISO 9001, COSMOS, RSPO and others you hold
  • Food-contact or compatibility data (packaging)
  • CE / safety compliance (machinery)

Questions

Asked by suppliers.

Is there a fee to join the supplier directory?

No. Listing is free for qualified suppliers.

Is the directory public?

No. It is used by our formulation, sourcing and project teams to select materials for client projects.

How long does qualification take?

It depends on the category and the documents you provide. Most suppliers with complete documentation are reviewed within a few weeks.

Do you guarantee orders?

No. Listing means your materials are considered first when they suit a brief; orders depend on each client project.
