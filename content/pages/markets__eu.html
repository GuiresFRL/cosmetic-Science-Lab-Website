Questions

Asked before we start.

Do I need an EU Responsible Person?

Yes. Every cosmetic sold in the EU needs a Responsible Person established in the EU, named on the label. We provide this service through our EU-established entity.

How long does it take to get on the EU market?

Around two to six weeks once your test data is complete. If stability or challenge tests are missing, add four to twelve weeks.

Can I use my EU file for the UK?

Most of it. The UK needs its own safety assessment, Responsible Person and SCPN notification, but we adapt the EU file rather than starting again.

What does it cost?

We quote per product after a formula review, because cost depends on ingredients, product type and the data you already hold.
