Fermented Cosmetic Ingredients from Guires CSL

At Guires Cosmetic Science Lab, CSL scientists develop fermented cosmetic ingredients and postbiotic actives at the Guires R&D Innovation Centre, from strain selection and 5 L trials to 100 L pilot fermentation, purification and freeze-dried powders, with every batch checked for identity and activity.

Results from the Guires R&D Innovation Centre support formulation decisions, ingredient selection and claims development. Where a market or retailer needs regulatory-grade data, such as GLP safety studies or accredited test reports, CSL arranges those studies as part of the project.

Questions

Asked before we start.

Can you work with our own strain?

Yes. CSL scientists can bank and ferment a strain you supply, under a confidentiality agreement, and the process stays yours.

What volumes can you make?

From laboratory flasks up to 100 L fermentor batches, enough for formulation, stability and consumer testing. Commercial volumes are transferred to a contract manufacturer.

Are biotech ingredients accepted in cosmetics?

Yes, in most markets, provided the ingredient has a safety assessment, a suitable INCI name and documentation. CSL regulatory specialists prepare this with you.
