The science

The botanical science we formulate for.

Natural formulas face three challenges: proving efficacy, keeping them stable and safe, and describing them honestly.

Botanical actives

Plant actives with clinical data.

A growing number of botanicals have been tested head-to-head against conventional actives. Bakuchiol has matched retinol for wrinkles with less irritation, and tea tree oil has reduced blemishes more gently than benzoyl peroxide. We build around those with the best evidence.

Oils & emollients

Traditional oils, measured results.

Plant oils and oatmeal are some of the oldest skin care ingredients, and several now have randomised trials behind them for dry and eczema-prone skin.

Natural oils oxidise. We add natural antioxidants, choose the right pack and test rancidity through shelf life.

Stable & safe

Natural, and built to last.

Natural and preservative-light formulas still need to pass challenge testing and stay stable. Waterless formats help, and every botanical needs a full safety review for allergens, phototoxicity and contaminants.

Market note

“Natural” and “clean” have no legal definition. We calculate natural-origin content under ISO 16128 and avoid free-from claims that disparage other products.

Clinical evidence

What the published trials show.

Peer-reviewed human studies behind the ingredients and approaches on this page. Each links to its PubMed record.

Randomised controlled trialAnti-ageing · bakuchiol

0.5% bakuchiol vs 0.5% retinol

Randomised, double-blind · 44 adults · 12 weeks

Both reduced wrinkle area and hyperpigmentation with no significant difference; bakuchiol caused less scaling and stinging.

Dhaliwal S et al. Br J Dermatol 2019;180(2):289–296 · PMID 29947134 ↗

Randomised controlled trialBlemishes · tea tree oil

5% tea tree oil gel vs 5% benzoyl peroxide

Randomised, single-blind · 124 people with acne · 3 months

Both reduced lesions significantly; tea tree worked more slowly but caused fewer side effects.

Bassett IB et al. Med J Aust 1990;153(8):455–458 · PMID 2145499 ↗

Randomised controlled trialDry, eczema-prone skin · coconut oil

Virgin coconut oil vs mineral oil

Randomised, double-blind · 117 children · 8 weeks

Eczema severity fell 68% with coconut oil vs 38% with mineral oil (P<0.05).

Evangelista MT et al. Int J Dermatol 2014;53(1):100–108 · PMID 24320105 ↗

Randomised controlled trialEczema-prone skin · colloidal oatmeal

1% colloidal oatmeal cream vs prescription barrier cream

Randomised, double-blind, active-controlled · children with eczema · 3 weeks

The oatmeal cream matched the prescription barrier cream on eczema improvement, with similar tolerability.

Manufacturer-sponsored.

Lisante TA et al. J Dermatolog Treat 2017;28(7):659–667 · PMID 28366039 ↗

Controlled trialSoothing · licorice

1% and 2% licorice extract gel vs base gel

Controlled three-group trial · 90 adults with eczema · 2 weeks

The 2% gel reduced redness, swelling and itch more than the 1% gel.

Saeedi M et al. J Dermatolog Treat 2003;14(3):153–157 · PMID 14522625 ↗

These are published studies on ingredients, methods and routines, not on products we have made. A finished product needs its own testing before a claim goes on pack, and we design that study with you. See the full evidence library

Claims review
Cosmetic or drug?

In India, products sold as Ayurvedic are Ayurvedic drugs licensed under AYUSH rules; herbal products sold as cosmetics fall under the Cosmetics Rules and cannot make treatment claims. Outside India they are regulated as cosmetics. We confirm the route before formulating.

FAQ

Clean, natural & Ayurvedic questions.

Can you make a COSMOS-certified or natural product?

Yes. We screen raw materials for the standard you choose, calculate natural-origin content under ISO 16128 and prepare the file your certifier asks for.

Can Ayurvedic formulas be sold abroad?

Yes, usually as cosmetics. We adapt the formula and claims to each market’s rules and ingredient lists.

Are natural products safer?

Not automatically. Botanicals can contain allergens and contaminants, so we run the same safety assessment as for any cosmetic.

Who owns the formula?

You do. We sign an NDA first, and the formula and technical file are handed over at the end.
