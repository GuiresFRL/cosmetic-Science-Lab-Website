The science

What drives blemishes and shine.

Excess sebum, blocked pores and inflammation combine to cause blemishes. We target each.

Oil control

Less shine, for longer.

Niacinamide reduced sebum output compared with placebo in randomised trials. Salicylic acid clears pores, and zinc and clays absorb surface oil.

Clinical evidence

What the published trials show.

Peer-reviewed human studies behind the actives and approaches on this page. Each links to its record.

Randomised controlled trialOil control · niacinamide

2% niacinamide vs placebo

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled · 100 adults · 4 weeks (plus a 30-person split-face study)

Sebum excretion fell significantly vs placebo at 2 and 4 weeks; casual sebum also fell in the split-face study.

Draelos ZD et al. J Cosmet Laser Ther 2006;8(2):96–101 · PMID 16766489 ↗

Randomised controlled trialBlemishes · tea tree oil

5% tea tree oil gel vs 5% benzoyl peroxide

Randomised, single-blind · 124 people with acne · 3 months

Both reduced lesions significantly; tea tree worked more slowly but caused fewer side effects.

Bassett IB et al. Med J Aust 1990;153(8):455–458 · PMID 2145499 ↗

Randomised controlled trialAcne face wash · tolerance

Reformulated 4% and 10% benzoyl peroxide washes vs an older wash

Randomised, evaluator-blind · 133 adults with acne · 21 days

The new washes were as well tolerated as the reference, with no meaningful change in irritation scores.

Benzoyl peroxide acne washes are OTC drugs in the US and medicines in the EU.

Santos-Caetano JP, Cargill MR. J Drugs Dermatol 2019;18(4):350–356 ↗

Controlled trialMakeup on acne-prone skin

Skin care and makeup guidance vs none, alongside acne treatment

Controlled comparison · 50 women with acne · 4 weeks

Makeup did not slow acne improvement, and the makeup group gained more on quality-of-life scores.

Matsuoka Y et al. J Dermatol 2006;33(11):745–752 · PMID 17073988 ↗

These are published studies on ingredients, methods and routines, not on products we have made. A finished product needs its own testing before a claim goes on pack, and we design that study with you. See the full evidence library

Cosmetic or drug?

In the US, products that claim to treat acne are OTC drugs with fixed active ingredients. In the EU and many other markets, "for blemish-prone skin" stays cosmetic. We word the claim for each market.

FAQ

Acne & oil control questions.

Can a cosmetic claim to treat acne?

Not in the US, where acne treatment claims make it an OTC drug. Cosmetic claims focus on blemish-prone skin, the appearance of blemishes and shine.

Does makeup make acne worse?

A controlled study found makeup did not slow acne improvement and improved quality of life. We design non-comedogenic textures and test them.

How do you test a non-comedogenic claim?

With a comedogenicity assessment on panels, and in-use studies on blemish-prone skin.

Who owns the formula?

You do. We sign an NDA before you share your brief, and the formula and technical file are handed over at the end of the project.
