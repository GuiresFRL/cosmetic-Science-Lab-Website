Questions

Asked before we start.

Do you offer private label or white label products?

We focus on custom formulation, so the formula is unique to your brand and owned by you.

Who owns the formula?

You do. The formula and its documentation belong to you, and we sign an NDA before you share your brief.

Can you match a product I already sell?

Yes. We reverse-engineer the texture and performance of a reference and build an independent formula that achieves it.

How many sample rounds are included?

The number of rounds is set in your proposal. Most projects settle on a formula within two to three rounds.

Can you work with my existing manufacturer?

Yes. We write the formula and manufacturing instructions to suit their equipment and hand over a tech-transfer pack.
