Questions

Asked before we start.

Can you prepare starter formulas for our trade-show launch?

Yes. CSL formulation chemists develop concept formulas around your ingredient and make samples, with INCI lists and procedures your customers can follow.

Will you publish the results?

Only with your written permission. Many suppliers use the reports for application notes and conference posters; publication-ready reports can be prepared on request.

Can you test our ingredient against a named competitor?

Yes. Competitor grades are bought on the open market and tested under the same conditions. Results are reported as measured, so they can be defended if questioned.

Do you support regulatory documentation?

CSL regulatory specialists check INCI naming and the status of the ingredient in your target markets, and can prepare the safety information brands need for their CPSR.
