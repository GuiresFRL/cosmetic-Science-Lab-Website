Results from the Guires R&D Innovation Centre support formulation decisions, ingredient selection and claims development. Where a market or retailer needs regulatory-grade data, such as GLP safety studies or accredited test reports, CSL arranges those studies as part of the project.

Questions

Asked before we start.

Does a negative result prove a product is halal?

It shows no porcine DNA was detected at the method’s sensitivity. Halal certification also depends on sourcing, processing and documentation, which CSL helps prepare.

Can DNA tests be run on finished creams?

Often, yes, but heavily processed ingredients may contain little intact DNA. For those, testing the raw materials is more reliable.

Which certifiers accept this data?

Certifiers set their own requirements. The data supports the certification file, and CSL can arrange testing at a laboratory a specific certifier recognises.
